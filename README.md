## 🚀 Selected Projects

### ⚡ Genki Dama: AI-Based Smart Energy Management for EVs
*Independent research concept*

An integrated energy-management architecture for electric vehicles that combines efficiency measures with solar, regenerative braking, vibration, and thermal energy pathways.

- Modular source-specific power paths with protection and a central Energy Management System (EMS) for isolated fault handling
- Multi-stage storage: primary battery, auxiliary battery, and supercapacitors
- AI-assisted framework for energy-use and trip-feasibility decisions
- Net-energy principle: a harvesting mechanism is kept only if the recovered energy justifies its losses, mass, complexity, and maintenance
- Staged validation plan (note: charging-interval figures are engineering targets, not demonstrated results)

### 📱 Attendance: Smart Offline Attendance System
*Cross-platform application*

Replaces paper attendance with QR-based session access, local proximity verification, and automated attendance management.

- Permanent QR code with an internal session credential that changes for each lecture
- Offline-first workflow with local communication and sync once internet is available
- BLE-based proximity verification to reduce remote and duplicate attendance
- Student and instructor workflows: courses, session activation, manual correction, and automatic attendance-rate and grade calculation
